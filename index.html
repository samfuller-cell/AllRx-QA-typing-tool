Methodology. Automated pass over the Directions Translated field against SOP §1.3 (Typing Workflow) requirements: an approved action (Take, Give, Instill, Apply, Inject, Inhale, Insert, Place, Dissolve, Unwrap, Use, Chew, Swallow, Rinse, etc.), a recognizable dosing frequency, and — for simple single-frequency oral solid sigs only — a Quantity ÷ (dose size × frequency) check against the Days Supply on file. PRN directions and multi-part/split regimens ("take X in the morning AND Y at bedtime") are excluded from the quantity math check to avoid false positives from free-text parsing; those still get flagged if the action or frequency itself is missing.
